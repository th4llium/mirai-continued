## 🌟 About

Mirai is a custom, vibrant visual shader designed to overhaul the atmosphere of **Minecraft: Bedrock Edition** with rich colors and enhanced lighting.

## ❓ How To Use & Troubleshooting

Most setup issues come from outdated loaders or graphics settings. Follow these steps to get Mirai running smoothly:

### 1. Use an Up-to-Date Loader
Make sure you are using a modern, supported loader for your platform. Outdated loaders will cause crashes or visual bugs.
* **Windows:** Recommended loaders include **Wyvern** or **BetterRenderDragon**. 
* **Android:** Recommended loaders include **MB Loader**.
* ⚠️ **Note:** *Minecraft Patched* is generally not recommended for this version.

### 2. Check Device Performance
Mirai pushes graphics further than the base game. Before installing, ensure your device can comfortably handle high-end visual configurations and vanilla shaders.

### 3. Proper Setup Steps (Graphics Workaround)
Due to how modern MCBE handles graphics pipelines, follow this exact order to prevent shader glitches:
1. Open your game and go to the main menu.
2. Set your graphics mode to **Fancy Graphics** from the main menu settings *before* joining a world.
3. Make sure the Mirai resource pack is active in your **Global Resources** or active world settings.
4. Load into your world.
5. Once you are successfully inside the world, you can switch your graphics mode to **Vibrant Visuals** and enjoy the shader!
